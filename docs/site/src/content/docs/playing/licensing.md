Luna Engine is **source available** under PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 with an additional **royalty-free commercial-game permission** from Peter McKinney (GitHub: [pmck91](https://github.com/pmck91)). These restrictions mean Luna is not licensed as OSI open source.

This page is a practical summary. The complete [LICENSE](/luna-engine/downloads/LICENSE) contains the unmodified base licence and Luna's additional permission; [NOTICE](/luna-engine/downloads/NOTICE) contains its attribution. The terms shipped with the version you use govern that version. Updating the repository does not rewrite an older release's licence or downloads. Third-party software and assets retain their own terms.

## Can I sell my game?

**Yes.** Individuals and businesses may use Luna to create, test, promote, publish, distribute, sell and play their games. No royalty or licence fee is payable to Peter McKinney for those activities under the game permission. Publishers and stores may distribute your game too.

You retain the rights you hold in your original story, dialogue, rules and assets. Using Luna does not require you to publish your game source or license your original content under Luna's terms. You still need the appropriate rights for other people's writing, artwork and assets. You may adapt Luna's supplied example game content and templates under the game permission, retaining their applicable notices; the original material remains its owners' work.

## Credit Luna

Games publicly distributed or made publicly playable under the additional game permission must include this credit and link in their game credits or accompanying documentation, where players can reasonably find them:

```text
Luna Engine by Peter McKinney (pmck91)
https://github.com/pmck91/luna-engine
```

No splash screen or logo placement is required. For a terminal game, an accompanying README is sufficient. There is no automatic credit insertion in the editor: include the credit when preparing your distribution. Credit is also encouraged for noncommercial games relying only on the base licence. All redistributed Luna software or supplied Luna material must retain the applicable required notices.

## Include the runner with a game

You may bundle the **unmodified Luna runner** with a paid or free game so that players can run it. Compiling an unmodified release for a platform, supplying configuration or adding a launch script does not itself modify the runner.

Keep these beside the runner, accessible to your players:

- The complete LICENSE, including the commercial-game permission.
- NOTICE, including Peter McKinney's required attribution.
- Applicable third-party licence and copyright notices for the software you distribute.

Use the legal files supplied with that runner version. Copying only the base PolyForm text omits Luna's game permission. Your game's own licence can cover your original content; it does not replace the terms for the bundled runner or third-party components.

Alternatively, distribute your `.luna` game with its credit and direct players to install Luna separately. A `.luna` export contains game data and registered assets; it does not automatically include the runner, legal sidecars or a credits file. Keep any notices required for reused material with your distribution.

## Commercialising Luna itself

The game permission does not authorise selling Luna itself, selling a modified engine/editor, distributing a modified runner commercially, or offering the engine/editor or a general-purpose runner as a paid hosted service. Those activities need separate written permission unless independently allowed by the base licence or applicable law. Charging for access to a particular authored game is covered by the game permission.

For licensing enquiries, contact [Peter McKinney through his GitHub profile](https://github.com/pmck91). The permission concerns rights he can license; it does not replace third-party licences or imply their authors endorse your game.
